PROFILE AND WAYPOINTS
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/PCTA_Centerline/FeatureServer : PCTA's authoritative centerline (built from Halfmile's 2018 release, reroutes through 2024, updated 01/2025); one polyline, 1,210,247 vertices, queried with outSR=4326. Supported: length 2,652.95 mi, the 0.01 mi sampling track, terminus coordinates.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/Tenthmile_Marker_2026/FeatureServer : 26,600 tenth-mile markers, lat/lon/mile, 26,558 with coordinates, last at mile 2655.8. Supported: profile x-axis and mile for every waypoint.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/PCT_Mile_Markers_2026/FeatureServer : 5,320 half-mile markers, 0.5 to 2,660 (5,311 with coordinates, last at 2655.5). Supported: cross-check of mileage.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/Halfmile_PCTWaypoints/FeatureServer : 8,526 Halfmile 2015 waypoints with names and lat/lon. Supported: coordinates of passes, highway crossings, PCTMidpoint, MexBorder, Monument78.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/Trail_Town_Resupply_Public/FeatureServer : 106 trail-town points. Supported: town coordinates, crow-fly distances, trail-towns item.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/PCT_Fires_and_Trail_Closures_public_view/FeatureServer : 144 closure entries 2006 to 2026. Supported: Dixie, Eagle Creek, Ptarmigan, closure counts.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services/PCT_Per_Thousand_Ft/FeatureServer : PCT miles per 1,000 ft band, 14 bands, total 2,662.9 mi. Supported: elevation-band items.
https://services5.arcgis.com/ZldHa25efPFpMmfB/ArcGIS/rest/services : the PCTA ArcGIS service directory, listing all layers. Also https://www.pcta.org/discover-the-trail/maps/pct-data/ (named in the service description as the download page; not opened).
https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer : USGS 3DEP getSamples, bare-earth DEM, metres converted to feet. Supported: every elevation in profile.json, profile_0p1mi.json, waypoints.json and the gain/loss calculations. Spot check, https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json (point query at Mount Whitney read 14,491 ft).
https://www.fs.usda.gov/trails/pacific-crest-nst/data-tools/gis : Forest Service page pointing to the PCTA centerline data (seen in search results, not opened).
https://pctmap.net/2014/03/04/elevation-gain-on-the-pacific-crest-trail/ : Halfmile, 489,418 ft gain, 488,411 ft loss, net 1,007 ft, per-section table, method (USGS 1/3 arc-second DEM, 5 m threshold, about 3/4 million points).
https://www.pcta.org/2014/new-numbers-elevation-gain-loss-pacific-crest-trail-17581/ : PCTA post of 2014-03-04 calling 489,418 ft the most accurate figure ever.
https://pctmap.net/2015/03/08/check-out-the-new-elevation-profiles/ : Halfmile 2015 elevation profile PDFs (California and Oregon/Washington); derivation of their elevations not stated.
https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/ : OSM geocoder, coordinates for Mount Whitney, San Jacinto Peak, Wanda Lake (Evolution Basin), Old Snowy Mountain, Rim Village (Crater Lake Lodge), Manning Park Resort, Bridge of the Gods.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Crest_Trail : length 2,653 mi, highest point Forester Pass 13,153 ft, lowest 140 ft (infobox) and about 110 ft (text), 25 national forests, 7 national parks, mid-way post 1,323.2 mi, completion rate about 14 percent.
https://www.backpacker.com/trips/the-halfway-point-of-the-pacific-crest-trail/ : 2016 account: monument at mile 1320.7, Halfmile suggested 1325.1.
Backpacker section guide listing the monument near 40.2129, -121.3534 (seen in search results, not opened).

https://www.pcta.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2024-PCT-Long-distance-Permit-numbers.png : the permit table 2013 to 2024, read from the image. The Total column does not equal the sum of the category columns.
https://www.pcta.org/discover-the-trail/thru-hiking-long-distance-hiking/2600-miler-list/ : 11,685 completions, 131 repeat; yearly counts taken by calling the page's own year selector (POST /wp-admin/admin-ajax.php, action pcta_milerlist) and counting rows; no names kept. 2020 completions are not recognised (18 listed); 2026 shows 1.
