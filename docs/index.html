Pacific Crest Trail

A footpath along the crest of the West, drawn in stars at the real positions of its towns and passes, under the sun and moon where you are right now.

From the Mexican border near Campo to Manning Park at the Canadian border, through California, Oregon and Washington. Most people who walk it all take about five months, April to September.

Burney Mountain Guest Ranch

A family ranch 0.8 miles off the trail, between Hat Creek Rim and Burney Falls. Bunks, camping, cabins, three meals a day, showers, laundry with loaner clothes, a hiker box, and a small resupply store. Open from May.

Resupply boxes by UPS or FedEx (USPS doesn't deliver there), marked with your name and the date you expect to arrive.

Years

  1. 1968Named one of the first two National Scenic Trails, with the Appalachian Trail.
  2. 1993Finished, with a golden spike in Soledad Canyon.
  3. 2017Big snow year. Fires closed the Eagle Creek route until 2021 and about 70 miles in Washington.
  4. 2020The PCTA asked hikers to stay home. 18 finishes reported.
  5. 2021The Dixie Fire closed about 130 miles, mile 1235 to 1365.
  6. 2023California snowpack reached 196% of average. Many hikers skipped the Sierra and came back.
  7. 2025Canada ended PCT entry permits.
